On the orthography

The Lesotho orthography used throughout this site was developed by missionaries of the Paris Evangelical Missionary Society — Thomas Arbousset, Eugène Casalis and Constant Gosselin — who arrived in 1833 and produced the first written form of Southern Sotho.

It differs from South African Sesotho in several regular ways: di/du becomes li/lu, kg becomes kh, tsh becomes tš, and w becomes o in certain positions. Grave accents mark open-mid vowels.

On the reliability of etymologies. The month derivations on this site are those directly attested in Mabille & Dieterlen (1950). Many popularly circulated explanations of Sesotho month names are folk etymology and are not necessarily accurate; nothing has been included here that the cited sources do not support. Where a source records a second reading, as with Mphalane, both are given.
